Everything you have planned, across every day, on one page.

## Adding a task

The field at the top of **Tasks** adds one. Beside it is the day the task is planned for — it starts on today, and you can change it before adding, so filing something for Friday does not mean going to Friday first.

:::callout{tone=info title="Added while a filter was on?"}
A brand new task is open and not overdue. If you add one while **Done** or **Overdue** is selected, the page switches to **To do** so you can see what you just added — rather than storing it correctly and appearing to do nothing.
:::

## The filters

| Filter {w-40} | What it lists |
| --- | --- |
| **To do** | Everything not yet ticked. |
| **Overdue** | Open tasks whose due date has passed. This is where work that slipped shows up. |
| **Done** | Completed tasks. |
| **All** | Everything, ticked or not. |

Filtering never deletes anything. The count beside the list is the total for the filter you are on, and **past due** is called out separately in red.

Search matches the task text and runs on the server, so it searches every task you have — not only the ones currently on screen.

## Working with a task

Each row carries everything you can do to it:

- **Tick** it to complete it. The completion time is recorded by the server, not by your browser.
- **Rename** it in place — click the text.
- **Move** it to another day with the date control on the row.
- **Delete** it. Deletes are soft: the row is recoverable for 30 days.

Tasks are grouped by the day they are planned for, newest day first, because the day is the unit people actually think in. An unscheduled task sits in its own group at the end.

:::callout{tone=warn title="Nothing carries itself over"}
A task planned for Monday that never got done stays on Monday. That is deliberate — moving it on your behalf would rewrite what actually happened that day, and "what did I get through last week?" would stop having an answer. Use **Overdue** to find that work, and move it yourself when you decide to.
:::

## Where tasks and notes meet

A task belongs to a day, and so does a note. Open **Open that day** on any day heading and you land on that day in [Notes & Calendar](/notes), where the day panel shows its appointments, its tasks and its note together.

Tasks are their own records — they are not buried inside a note — which is why this page can answer "what is still open?" across every day at once.
